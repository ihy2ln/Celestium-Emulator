# Phone controls

The **Controls** tab works on a running device.

## Buttons

Back, Home, Recents, Power, Volume +/−, Rotate and Screenshot. Foldable devices also get Fold and Unfold.

## Location

Pick a city or type a latitude and longitude, then press **Set location**. Apps that use GPS see the new position immediately.

## Battery

Drag the slider to set the battery level. Turn **Charging** on or off to test how apps react.

## Network

- **Mobile data speed:** 5G (unlimited), LTE, 3G, EDGE or GPRS.
- **Wi-Fi** and **Airplane mode** switches.
- **HTTP proxy:** enter `host:port`. From inside the emulator, your PC is `10.0.2.2`, so a proxy such as Fiddler or Charles on your PC is `10.0.2.2:8888`. **Clear proxy** removes it.

## Display and system

- **Dark theme:** Android's system dark mode.
- **Gesture navigation:** swipe gestures on, or the 3-button bar when off.
- **Animations:** Normal, Fast (0.5×) or Off. Off makes UI tests faster and more reliable.

## Home screen and style

- **Set wallpaper from PC…** copies an image to the phone and opens Android's own wallpaper screen, so you can crop it and choose home or lock screen.
- **Font size** and **Display size.**
- **Get a launcher:** one click opens Lawnchair, Nova, Niagara or Smart Launcher in the Play Store. This needs a device with the Play Store, such as Games.

## Sensors

- **Shake** sends a short shake gesture.
- **Touch fingerprint** simulates a registered finger on the fingerprint sensor. Enroll a fingerprint in Android's Settings first.
