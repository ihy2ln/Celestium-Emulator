# Apps and files

## Installing apps

There are three ways:

- Drag `.apk` files anywhere onto the Celestium window.
- Press **Install APK…** in the top bar.
- Press **Install APK…** on the **Apps** tab.

The app goes to the selected device if it's running. Otherwise Celestium asks which running device to use.

## The Apps tab

Lists the apps you've installed. Turn on **System apps** to see everything. Search filters by name. Double-click an app to open it, or select it and use the buttons:

- **Open** and **Force stop.**
- **App info** opens Android's settings page for the app.
- **Save APK…** copies the installed APK to your PC.
- **Clear data…** resets the app to a fresh install.
- **Uninstall…**

## The Files tab

Browse the phone's storage, starting at `/sdcard`. Double-click a folder to open it. **↑ Up**, **Storage**, **Downloads** and **Pictures** jump around.

- **Upload files…**, or drag files onto the list, to copy them into the folder you're viewing.
- **Download to PC** copies the selected items to `Downloads\Celestium` on your PC.
- **New folder…** and **Delete…**

Dropping a non-APK file onto the main window sends it to the phone's **Download** folder.

## Screenshots and recordings

- **Screenshot:** on Overview or Controls, from the tray, or with Ctrl+S.
- **Record screen:** on Overview or from the tray. Press it again to stop; recordings stop by themselves after 30 minutes.

Screenshots and recordings are saved to `Pictures\Celestium`. You can change the folder in **⋯ › Settings**, and **Media folder** opens it.
